WHY WE'RE DOING THIS

A small gift can have a long life.

Our hope is to give the children more than money to spend today. We want to help create a financial foundation they can benefit from later in life.

By investing gifts over many years, even modest contributions have time to participate in the power of long-term compounding. Investment values can rise and fall, so growth is not guaranteed—but time can be a powerful advantage.
